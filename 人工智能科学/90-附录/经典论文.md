## 3. 概率统计与统计学习

### 教材

1. Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman. *The Elements of Statistical Learning*.
2. Bishop. *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning*.
3. Vapnik. *Statistical Learning Theory*.
4. Shalev-Shwartz, Ben-David. *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*.

## 4. 深度学习与优化

### 教材

1. Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville. *Deep Learning*.
2. Nocedal, Wright. *Numerical Optimization*.
3. Boyd, Vandenberghe. *Convex Optimization*.

### 代表论文

1. Krizhevsky et al. *ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks*.
2. He et al. *Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition*.
3. Kingma & Ba. *Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization*.

## 5. 强化学习

### 教材

1. Sutton & Barto. *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*.
2. Bertsekas. *Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control*.

### 代表论文

1. Mnih et al. *Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning*.
2. Schulman et al. *Trust Region Policy Optimization*.
3. Schulman et al. *Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms*.
4. Lillicrap et al. *Continuous Control with Deep Reinforcement Learning*.
5. Haarnoja et al. *Soft Actor-Critic*.
6. Espeholt et al. *IMPALA*.

## 6. Transformer 与大模型

### 代表论文

1. Vaswani et al. *Attention Is All You Need*.
2. Devlin et al. *BERT*.
3. Brown et al. *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners*.
4. Lewis et al. *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks*.

## 7. 图神经网络

1. Kipf & Welling. *Semi-Supervised Classification with Graph Convolutional Networks*.
2. Hamilton et al. *Inductive Representation Learning on Large Graphs*.
3. Veličković et al. *Graph Attention Networks*.
